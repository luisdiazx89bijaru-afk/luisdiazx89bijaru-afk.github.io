The inscription that recorded the fall of Mithras

Archaeologists report a mysterious inscription at an underground Roman sanctuary in Zerzevan Castle mentioning both Mithras and Jesus Christ.

WorldNexus DeskEarly report5 min read

Zerzevan Castle is the setting described in the initial report.

Deep inside an underground Roman sanctuary at Zerzevan Castle, researchers decoded a mysterious inscription mentioning both Mithras and Jesus Christ.

The discovery raises questions about the religious world of the late Roman period and how beliefs met in shared places. The inscription and its interpretation deserve careful examination alongside the archaeological record.
